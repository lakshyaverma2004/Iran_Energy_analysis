[PROJECT FINDING]: Brent crude posted a cumulative abnormal return of +30.63% in 9 trading days following the February 28 strikes (3.4%/day). By contrast, the de-escalation around the June 17 MoU was largely priced-in beforehand: Brent shed 24.2% of abnormal gains over 10 pre-event trading days (2.4%/day), with only +0.19% incremental movement on the signing day itself — escalation was 1.4x faster than de-escalation. Natural Gas moved less sharply than crude oil, with a peak CAR of +17.87%.
